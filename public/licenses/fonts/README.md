These presets are typographic genre references. They do not include game logos, wordmarks, screenshots, proprietary font files, or a claim of affiliation with any game publisher.

## Important distinction

The Pricedown typeface associated with the Grand Theft Auto wordmark is not bundled. A free desktop-use license is not automatically a webfont or software-embedding license. PeakScore uses independently designed, SIL-OFL-licensed families instead.

The SIL Open Font License permits embedding and redistribution with software when its conditions are followed. Do not sell the font files separately. Keep the copyright notice and license with every distributed copy. If a font is modified, check reserved-name requirements and do not imply author endorsement.
